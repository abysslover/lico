## What is true before this phase

- The first step of New session paints four pictures over the blank lines of the panel's body: Claude, Codex, Local LLM, Terminal (`PICKS` in `newsession_wizard.rs`, drawn by `picker.rs`; each is a square of the same size, at most 112 logical pixels, with a word under it where it is chosen). Claude and Codex are grey when no account of that provider exists, with the notice "needs an account first"; Local LLM and Terminal are never grey. The Terminal picture opens a shell at once in the folder on the left and closes the panel (`M23-P03a`, `M23-P03d`).
- A terminal tab shows the picture of `claude`, `codex` or `agy` while that program runs in it (P04a), and the conversations of `agy` are cards in Chats (P04b). A person who wants `agy` starts a Terminal and types `agy`: nothing in New session knows it.

## Results (2026-11-20)

- **What the person sees.** The first step of New session has five pictures of one size: Claude, `agy` (the chevron over a dot, the picture of its tab), Codex, Local LLM, Terminal. `agy` is lit when a program named `agy` is found on this machine and grey, with the sentence "agy is not installed on this machine." (in the lines under the pictures and when it is clicked), when not; it asks for no account. A click on the lit picture closes the panel and opens a terminal in the folder on the left, as the Terminal picture does, and the window types `agy` and Enter into it; the tab then shows the `agy` picture (P04a). Nothing else is typed: never the flag that skips permissions.
- **What is found.** An executable regular file named `agy` (a link to one counts) in an absolute folder of the window's own `PATH`, or in `~/.local/bin` or `~/bin`; looked for each time the wizard opens (`agy_look`), never at a frame. A relative entry of the `PATH` is not looked in (it is the folder the window happens to be in), nor is a folder named `agy`, a file without an execute bit, or a link to nothing. An alias or a function of the shell is not found; Terminal remains.
- **What the focus does.** The first focus of a form that opened on a provider is still that provider's picture, never `agy` (it is no provider of a session: `PROVIDERS` stays Claude, Codex, Local LLM); back from a step the picture that was chosen has the focus; the arrows go round five. With no account at all Local LLM has the focus (the fourth place now).
- **Tests first and red.** Unit tests: the order and the words of the pictures, `agy` grey with its own reason and no word of an account, lit when found with no account, the focus over five (first focus, back, round), the click on a found `agy` (panel closed, a terminal asked for), the search (`PATH` order, the two folders of the home and no other, a file that cannot run, a folder of that name, a dangling link, a link to a program, a relative entry that resolves from here), the line typed (`agy` and Enter, no conversation), `tile_rects` for five. Two window tests (`tests/test_agy_tile.py`: the picture is second and lit, a fake `agy` started plain in the folder on the left and the tab shows its picture, the focus with no account is on Local LLM; and a file that cannot run and a folder of that name are no program, a grey picture says why and opens nothing, `~/.local/bin` is looked in each time, grey again when it is gone); the window tests that index the pictures (`test_icon_toolbar_wizard.py`, `test_terminal_tab.py`) take the new order, and `test_login_window.py` no longer assumes anything of the machine's own `agy` (it is the one test that does not run in a private home and `PATH`).
- **Mutation checks.** 29 mutation checks caught (21 unit, 8 window; the survivors of each level were fixed or covered by a test)
- **Gates.** The changed-files suite at the commit: 250 tests in 79 files, 250 passed in 129.4 s (peak 955.7 MiB) on a quiet machine; the two tries before were 250 of 250 too but over the budget of two other tests. `cargo xtask check` passes. `cargo clippy --all-targets` gives the same findings as at the parent (139 warnings and 5 errors on the program, 155 and 6 on the tests). The guide is 1.14.0. Found while testing: a window test that does not run in a private home and `PATH` (`test_login_window.py`) saw the machine's own `agy` and had to stop assuming anything of it; a mutation survived at the window level (the picture drawn as the Local LLM one) until a pixel check was added; and the commit was refused twice for the budget of two tests that have nothing to do with the change (the busy disk of the machine made the hidden-character check take 97 s alone), so the hook was run again when the disk was quiet (neither the budget nor the hook was touched).
- **Limits** (the guide says them; the owner may want them changed). The picture says `agy`, the command's name, not the product's; `agy` is looked for on this machine only (the Terminal picture on an ssh host is not offered either); a program that is on the shell's `PATH` but not on the window's and not in the two folders of the home shows grey, and Terminal then starts it by hand; `agy` is started as a plain terminal command, so it is the person's own `agy`, not a session of Lico's (level C, later).
