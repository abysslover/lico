## What was found (2026-11-24)

- The menu entries' `key` field is a label only: no key is resolved through the menu table (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+Shift+W, Alt+digits and the others have handlers of their own), so a line can leave the menu without taking its key with it.
- `menu_entries` was the one function every consumer read (the dropdown, the dump, the tests): the diet is made there, over the unchanged table of the old four menus (`all_entries`), so each entry's reason (why it cannot act now) is still computed by the code that always computed it.
- `Cmd::NewSessionOptions`, `Cmd::NewTerminal` and `Cmd::GoTo` had no other caller: the variants and their handlers are removed.
- Nearly fifty window tests entered the menus by label (`menu_click`, `menu_reveal`, `client_reveal`) or by a key (`alt+h`, `alt+v`): they needed a map, said once in `tests/gui_helpers.py` (an entry that is not on the menu asked for is looked for on `More`), and the tests of lines that are gone use the keys instead.

| D3 | `More` is a fifth top-level menu with the mnemonic **Alt+M**, its groups as before (`group_of` keeps only the six groups `More` holds) | "reachable from one place"; no new search window to build; the menu tree machinery of `M22-P04a` is reused |

| D5 | The reasons of the lines that left the menus are still computed (`all_entries` is `pub(crate)`) and tested there | the rule of when `Allow` waits for its dwell or `Close` is refused is the code's, not the menu's |
