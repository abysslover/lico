## 4. Found on the way (READ, MEASURED)

- **The suite is over the limit of `180 s`** also on a quiet machine (`199.4 s` at a load of `6`). The limit stays (the owner's); whether to raise it, or to cut the cost by splitting the modules so that finished ones are not tested again (the owner's M26 of 2026-11-26), is the owner's decision. Every phase of M25 hit it in its by-hand run (`183.3 .. 196.5 s`) and was accepted by the hook (limit `300 s`), never by raising anything.
- **The first build after a version change peaks over `1,536 MiB`** (`1,725.2 MiB`), as at `0.8.2`; P02 had one too (`2,083.1 MiB`, the new `agy` modules and the first test build) and P05 `1,722.4 MiB`. The bound stays; the recovery is to run the suite by hand once on the staged tree, after which the build is skipped by hash.
- **A file crossed the ratchet of `800` lines in P02** (`folders.rs`, `816`): the driver-kind checks moved into `new_session::prepare_driver_kind` (`792`) rather than the ratchet being raised.
- **A stale test voided a mutation round** (P04): a P02 test that was already red was found by the first mutation round of a later phase; the round was stopped, the test changed and the round redone. The lesson is to run the unmutated tests first (the mutation script of P06 says so in its header).
- **Flaky wait under load**: one window test (`test_tool_create`) failed once at P05 (passes alone three times; the second run `600/600` in `183.3 s`).
- **No hook was bypassed** and no limit raised: the refusals of M25 for a build peak over the bound (P02, P05 and this release) were answered by running the suite by hand and committing with the receipts.
- **The guide had three sentences of levels A and B** that said the **agy** picture and a card open a terminal; they were corrected in P07.

## 5. What is the owner's (not decided here)

- **G3 for M16 to M25, with the one real turn of `agy`**: start a session in the window, sign in in a Terminal tab if the program asks, give it one turn in `default`, answer one real permission request, close the window, reopen it and resume the conversation. The security note's gaps `1`, `3`, `4` and `10` are read against what happens. Nothing of this was run: the real program was started only to record what `initialize` and `session/new` answer, with an empty home and no sign-in.
- **Choices made for the owner, open to change**: the telemetry flag (`--disable-telemetry` is always passed); `auto` for `agy` answers `allow_once` for every request, and the window starts every `agy` session in `default` (decision D5 as amended in P03); the environment of seven names means a key exported in the shell does not reach the program; a spawn failure is told as `busy`; `session.create kind=agy` without a folder uses a scratch workspace; there is no `session/cancel` (a turn cannot be stopped from the window); a conversation whose folder is gone resumes in a workspace of the host; the terminal path of levels A and B for a conversation was removed in P05; decision D10 was read as "no picker of the program's own modes" (the permission switch of the Session menu works for `agy`); an `agy` answer can send to another session by a `@name` paragraph (the approval card or the kept room is the brake); handing over a whole `agy` conversation is not supported (as for Codex); attachments are refused for `agy`.
- **The limit of `180 s`** and the cost of the suite (above); M26 (the module split, by `/refactor`, a plan first).
- **From the earlier releases** (see the [`0.8.2` report](20261126_07_M24-P12_release_0_8_2_report.md) section 5): the `[PRI-HANDOFF]` prompts that some agents print into a conversation; no Trash for Delete in Directories; archives that hold a link are refused; the colour constants of the transcript view; windows narrower than `70` text columns show no time; the scope of the file link; `tests/test_session_memory.py` depends on the host's load; older middle dots (the pill of a tool call, the chat cards, the Call Room titles, the Home tab's prefix, a title in `mention.rs`, `doc/index_4.md`) that the owner's rule of 2026-11-26 leaves in unmodified code.
