## 2. The scale-2 window peak

P07 measured one shown tab at scale 2 at `42.6 MiB` steady and `50.4 MiB` peak (0.4 over the gate). The peak is the moment a frame is decoded: the decoder keeps its scratch (the raw rows) beside the pixels, two pictures of memory at once. Releasing the old picture before decoding the new one (done) did not move it (`50.2`); the cheapest honest means that does is to ask the page for less: **at scale 2 and more the window asks for at most `1400 x 900` pixels** (scale 1 keeps `1920 x 1200`), and the tab says "the page is drawn at 1400 x 900". Measured again with the real engine: **steady `39.4`, peak `44.1 MiB`** at the default size, scale 2. A window that is already `3840 x 2160` is at `50.3 MiB` before any tab (a tab adds `6.0`, peak `61.0`). **The worst case at the frame cap stays as said (COMPUTED over 50 MiB at scale 2, not measured).** The better fix, a decoder that writes pixels row by row without the raw copy, is a change of `lico-frame` that I did not make; the owner may want it.
